# Positioning

Purpose: Unique advantage, category, alternatives, promises, reasons to believe and prohibited claims.

## Accepted knowledge

- **Positioning statement (verbatim, fase-2/02 §1):** *«Per le famiglie, gli anziani e chi cerca un approccio naturale alla salute nel Bellinzonese, Farmacia Vitalis è la farmacia indipendente di Lumino che unisce la farmacia classica e la medicina naturale con servizi accessibili 24/7, perché — a differenza delle grandi catene — ci prendiamo il tempo per conoscerti e seguirti come persona, non come scontrino.»*
- **Official tagline:** *«Ci prendiamo il tempo per te.»* — EN "We make time for you.", SR "Uvek imamo vremena za tebe." (fase-2/02, fase-3/02).
- **Internal synthetic positioning:** "La farmacia di fiducia che unisce natura e innovazione" (validated by ANALISI_COMPETITIVA.md — the "independent + innovative + natural" quadrant is empty in the 0–10 km area).
- **4 message pillars (fase-2/02 §2)** — every content piece must lean on at least one: 1) Tempo e fiducia (vs chains), 2) Natura + Scienza sotto lo stesso tetto, 3) Sempre accessibile (locker 24/7 + vending — the most newsworthy launch message), 4) Il cuore del Centro Opti (families/ecosystem).
- **Narrative enemy:** not a named competitor but "la farmacia impersonale delle catene". Rule: **never name competitors publicly**; differentiation stays implicit ("non siamo una catena", "primo e unico nella zona") (fase-2/02 §6).
- **Boilerplate 50 and 100 words** ready in fase-2/02 §5 (press/media-kit use). Elevator pitches (30s and 10s) in §4.
- **Competitive map (ANALISI_COMPETITIVA.md, Apr 2026):** direct: Farmacia Castione/Pedroni (chain, 17 sites, digital score 4/10, IG 424 followers/15 posts), **Farmacia Moesa** (opened Sep 2024, Arbed Smart Center Castione, Spazio Salute, most similar concept, 5/10 — monitor monthly), Pedroni Camorino (~5–6 km). Benchmarks: **Farmacia Tosi** Gravesano (natural + social gold standard, 7.5/10, Dr. Tosi as brand ambassador, 4.9/5 Google), Farmacia Viganello (galenic lab since 1955, 5/10). "Farmacia Chiusi" not found online — verify with Paride.
- **Unique services no competitor has:** locker 24/7, gluten-free foods, kids' gifts corner, and their combination with natural medicine + own-label preparations (ANALISI_COMPETITIVA.md §3).
- **Known weaknesses to mitigate:** shorter hours than Pedroni Castione (closed midday, Saturday afternoon, Sunday) — mitigation rule: every mention of opening hours must also mention locker + vending machine (fase-2/04 §6); zero brand awareness at start; recommended: free home delivery to evaluate, brand ambassador to name, 20 Google reviews in month 1 (ANALISI_COMPETITIVA.md §7–8).
- **Chromatic differentiation:** avoid pure pastel mint green (too close to Moesa), institutional green (Pedroni), green+navy (Tosi); Vitalis takes "pastel green + warm color" territory inspired by **Sunday Natural (sunday.de)** — client Paride's stated packaging benchmark (ANALISI_COMPETITIVA.md §11, §13).
- **Prohibited claims (health-sector, see operations.md for the full rules):** no advertising of prescription or cassa-malati-reimbursed medicines, no therapeutic claims ("supporta il benessere", never "cura/guarisce/previene"), no health-condition targeting or personal attributes in ads (fase-2/02 §7–8, fase-2/07 §6).
- **SEO positioning:** own "farmacia Lumino" (zero competition — first mover wins), then expand to "farmacia naturale Bellinzona", "Apotheke Lumino", "alimenti senza glutine Lumino", "locker farmacia Ticino" (ANALISI_COMPETITIVA.md §6).
- **Launch concept:** countdown «Il tempo sta per cominciare», series «Cosa troverai da noi che non trovi altrove», hashtag #VitalisInArrivo, list «Vitalis in anteprima» (fase-2/06).
- **Landing hero (recommended variant A):** "A settembre, a Lumino apre una farmacia che ti conosce per nome." (fase-3/02 §1a).
