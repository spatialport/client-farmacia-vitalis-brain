# Offer

Purpose: Products/services, economics, proof, differentiation and current commercial priorities.

## Proposed knowledge (awaiting review by Alex)

### Core services
- Prescription and OTC medicines; parapharmacy and dermocosmetics; natural medicine and homeopathy; phytotherapy; supplements; **gluten-free foods** (not available anywhere in the area, including Denner) (PROJECT_v2.md §4).
- Health services: blood-pressure measurement; compression-stockings fitting (annual check free via cassa malati); wound dressing; vaccinations; pharmacist check-up (billable to cassa malati — details to confirm); sale (not rental) of medical equipment (PROJECT_v2.md §4).

### Differentiating services (unique in the primary area, per ANALISI_COMPETITIVA.md §3)
- **Locker 24/7** for medicine pickup — first in the area; supplier via Daniele Pronzini; free service (PROJECT_v2.md, fase-2/04 §2.7).
- **Emergency vending machine** (pacifiers, baby bottles, disinfectant, plasters, sun cream) — strategic for THI LAND families (PROJECT_v2.md, fase-2/04 §2.8).
- **Free naturopath consultation** 10–15 min in-store, private room; paid in-studio follow-up `[TARIFFA — da cliente]` (PROJECT_v2.md, fase-2/04 §2.1).
- **Preparazioni magistrali a marchio Farmacia Vitalis** — ~10 own-label products at launch (magnesium, propolis, …); own labels on bottles (PROJECT_v2.md, fase-2/04 §2.6).
- **Prescriptions via WhatsApp** (photo of ricetta → prepared → pickup at counter or locker) — privacy/compliance flow still to validate (PROJECT_v2.md §4, fase-3/04 §6.1).
- **Kids' toys & gifts corner** ("L'Angolo delle Sorprese") — only one in the area, synergistic with THI LAND birthday parties (PROJECT_v2.md, fase-2/04 §3).
- **Private consultation room** ("saletta") and monthly themed days ("Le Giornate Vitalis": Belotti hearing checks, skin/hair analysis, nutrition, compression stockings) (PROJECT_v2.md §4, fase-2/04 §2.10).

### Packaged offer architecture (fase-2/04)
- 10 named packages in 4 families: **«Prenditi un Momento»** (Un Quarto d'Ora per Te · Il Punto della Situazione ⚠ · Preparato da Noi, per Te), **«Tieni Tutto Sotto Controllo»** (Misura il Tuo Benessere · Gambe Leggere · Pronto Intervento Vitalis ⚠ · Le Giornate Vitalis), **«Sempre Qui per Te»** (Il Locker che Non Dorme Mai · SOS Genitori · La Ricetta? Mandacela su WhatsApp ⚠), **«Il Mondo Vitalis»** (Angolo delle Sorprese · Scaffale Senza Glutine). Package names must be used consistently on every touchpoint (fase-2/04 §6).
- ⚠ = packages whose public communication is blocked pending health-law validation (check-up cassa malati, vaccinations, WhatsApp prescriptions).
- All pricing is placeholder `[TARIFFA — da cliente]`; no tariffs have been provided yet (fase-2/04 placeholder table).

### Not offered
- No in-house nutrition consulting (no nutritionist on staff; external partner planned) and no home delivery yet (to evaluate later; competitors Tosi/Moesa offer it free — flagged gap) (PROJECT_v2.md §4, ANALISI_COMPETITIVA.md §7).
- No e-commerce ("la farmacia non vende online") — ADV works on local awareness and leads, not sales (fase-2/12).

### Launch offer (September 2026)
- "Gira la ruota" prize wheel (everyone wins; 1 spin per person; spin requires leaving a contact), 10–15% vouchers on free-sale products only (30-day validity, prescription/reimbursed products explicitly excluded), kids' giveaways 150–200 pcs, small refreshment with a signposted gluten-free option (fase-2/06 §3, §5.8).
