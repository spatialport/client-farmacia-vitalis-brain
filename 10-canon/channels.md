## Accepted knowledge

### Channel architecture (fase-2/01 §5)
- **Google Business Profile** — #1 channel for a pharmacy; weekly posts/photos/Q&A; answer ALL reviews within 48h; absolute first priority: register/verify on "farmacia Lumino" (zero competition).
- **Landing / site (www.farmaciavitalis.ch)** — conversion hub: lead form (segmented select → Vitalis Mail), Calendly booking (fallback WhatsApp), trilingual IT/EN/SR, countdown; live July 2026 (fase-3).
- **Instagram** — primary social; contract Fase 5: **8 feed posts + 8 reels/month (16 contents) + stories 3–5/week**, publishing window 17:30–19:30, max 1 content/day. 6 fixed rubriche: Dietro il bancone, Natura & Scienza, Solo da noi, Vita da Centro Opti, Di stagione, Parola vostra; 8 recurring reel formats (fase-2/08).
- **Facebook** — selective mirror with softer copy (older audience), FB Events, measured organic posts in local Bellinzonese groups (max 1–2/month per group) (fase-2/08 §2).
- **WhatsApp** — direct channel: prescriptions via photo, community channel «Vitalis in anteprima», max 1 broadcast/week ("mai spam" rule; test: "Questo a un vicino di casa lo manderesti?") (fase-2/06 §4).
- **Email (Vitalis Mail — internal system built by Spatial Port, not Brevo)** — one master list "Vitalis — Community" + attribute-based segments; flows: Welcome (3 emails written), seasonal (Difese d'autunno, Natale), retention (+2 days, review ask), reorder (opt-in per product), newsletter 2/month from Oct 2026. Targets: 300+ subscribers pre-opening, 800+ by Aug 2027, open rate >35% (fase-2/09). Note: PROJECT_v2 originally planned Brevo/Mailchimp; superseded by Vitalis Mail.
- **Paid (Meta + Google)** — 3 flights: F0 pre-launch Jul–Aug (Meta 80/85%, Google brand-only), F1 launch Sep–Oct (burst "siamo aperti" + event), F2 consolidation Nov–Feb (Natale, retargeting). **Budget ceiling confirmed by client: CHF 600/month** (peak Sep–Oct only), regime ~CHF 400/month until Feb 2027; 14-month plan totals CHF 6,100 (Meta 3,870 / Google 2,230). Naming convention `VIT_{PIATTAFORMA}_{FLIGHT}_{OBIETTIVO}_{AUDIENCE}`; CPL benchmarks: Meta < CHF 8 (F0) (fase-2/07, fase-2/12 v2.0, fase-4/KIT-ADV).
- **PR local** — main news angle: "il primo locker farmaceutico 24/7 di Lumino e dintorni"; 2 ready press releases (apertura + innovazione locker); prioritized Ticino media list (laRegione, Corriere del Ticino, tio.ch, Ticinonews/Teleticino 🔴; RSI, TicinoToday, Radio Ticino/3i 🟡); press corner at opening; press contact = Alex Bellesia (fase-2/10).
- **No TikTok/LinkedIn in the first 6 months**; no Display/YouTube campaigns (PMax covers marginally) (fase-2/08 §2, fase-2/07 §3.7).
- **Physical → digital:** one QR everywhere (billboard 4×2, shop-window film, partner cards) → landing bridge page → WhatsApp channel or email form (fase-2/06 §4.1).
