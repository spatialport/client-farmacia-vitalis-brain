### Channel architecture (fase-2/01 §5)
- **Google Business Profile** — #1 channel for a pharmacy; weekly posts/photos/Q&A; answer ALL reviews within 48h; absolute first priority: register/verify on "farmacia Lumino" (zero competition).

- **Instagram** — primary social. **Editorial system = "La strategia social" v3 (15 Sep 2026, evidence `farmacia-vitalis-ev-2026-09-15-strategia-social-v3`)**, which supersedes the 6 rubriche / 8 reel formats of fase-2/08 for everything created from October 2026 on. Function: make people who already heard of the pharmacy *choose* it (trust), not discovery. Three surfaces, three roles: feed = why you trust us · Stories = why you come by today · newsletter = why you buy. **Twelve recurring formats** (fixed structure, variable content; no format ⑦ — the local thread was cut): ① La prima domanda (signature, Green, 3/mo) · ② A volte no (Yellow, 2/mo) · ③ Dipende (Yellow, 1/mo, alternating months with ⑥) · ④ Chi trovi al banco (Green, 2/mo) · ⑤ Sta prendendo forma (Green, 3/mo, pre-opening only, closes on opening day) · ⑥ Come funziona (Green, 1/mo) · ⑧ Questo mese al banco, A farmaceutico / B naturale (Yellow, 1/mo) · ⑨ Lo sapevi che…? (Yellow, 4/mo) · ⑩ Vero o falso? (Yellow, 2/mo) · ⑪ La scelta di [nome] (Yellow, 1/mo) · ⑫ Solo questa settimana (Yellow, 1–2/mo) · ⑬ Vinci con Vitalis (Yellow, 1/mo). Rhythm: **~13 feed contents/month (3/week) from one production day/month** + daily Stories outside the count (rituals: Chi c'è oggi · La domanda di oggi · Risposta rapida · Dal banco) + 6 Highlights (Chi siamo, Il metodo, Domande, Servizi, Curiosità, Novità; no "patologie"). Contract Fase 5 still reads 8 posts + 8 reels/month: in October 2026 the gap is covered by launch-event contents outside the count; from November the rhythm is to be confirmed. Publishing window 17:30–19:30, max 1 content/day. **Commercial load:** feed ≤5% promo · Stories 20–25% · newsletter is the home of promotions · promos run as ads, never in the feed. **Practical test for every piece:** first second = product → catalogue; first second = the question or the criterion, product as the answer → Vitalis. Hooks are the first spoken sentence and promise only what the next twenty seconds deliver.

### Social governance (strategia v3, decision 2)
- **Every feed post is approved by the pharmacy before publishing, no exceptions** (universal model). Three-tier classification of what is said: **Green** = method, process, the questions we ask, services, territory, people (fast approval) · **Yellow** = product categories, general guidance, seasonality, "when it makes sense and when not" (approval with a point-by-point check of products and indications) · **Red** = specific pathologies, dosages, interactions, efficacy claims, pregnancy and paediatrics (**never published**). Formats are designed on Green so the approval bottleneck never appears.
- Territory wording: "siamo a Lumino" (a fact), never "la farmacia dei luminesi" (an exclusive belonging that cuts out half the catchment).
- Pre-opening proof objects (one per content, minimum): proof of person, proof of method, material proof (site, shelves, locker, first order), proof of choice (what we keep and what not, and why). A content with none of these is a promise, and promises don't accumulate.
