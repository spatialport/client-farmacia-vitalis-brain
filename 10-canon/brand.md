## Accepted knowledge

### Brand truth
- Purpose: a proximity pharmacy that gives people time — "la salute ha bisogno di tempo e di facce che ti conoscono" (fase-2/10 quote draft).
- Belief: you shouldn't have to choose between classic pharmacy and natural medicine; independence beats chain logic (fase-2/02).
- Promise / tagline: *«Ci prendiamo il tempo per te.»* (discovery, PROJECT_v2; official in brandbook v6).
- Reasons to believe: stable 4-person team, saletta riservata, free naturopath consultation, own-label preparazioni magistrali, locker 24/7, kids' corner, gluten-free shelf (fase-2/02 pillar proofs).
- Naming detail: logo is the version "con stanghettina/lineetta", chosen for legibility for the elderly target (PROJECT_v2 §6). Asset: logo-orizzontale-colore.png.

### Visual identity — tokens (brandbook-v6)
- Colors: Lime `#ACC90F` (primary/accents) · Green `#6FA627` · Forest `#519633` · Deep Green `#256B2E` · Honey `#D4A76A` (warm accent) · Sand `#E8D5B7` · Cream `#F5EDE0` · Off-White `#F8F6F1` (background) · Charcoal `#575656` (text) · Warm Gray `#8A8A8A` (MASTER_PROMPT §5).
- **70/30 rule:** warm tones (cream/sand/off-white) dominate; greens are accents. Never lime as full background; never "verde ospedale" pages (fase-2/08 §4, fase-3/01).
- Typography: **Urbanist** (300/400/500/600) for titles/UI/body; **Instrument Serif italic** for claims and emotional accents (MASTER_PROMPT §5).
- Elements: thin double-helix DNA pattern at 5–7% opacity, pharmaceutical cross iconography, rounded corners 16–24px, delicate shadows (MASTER_PROMPT §5). Note: the landing v2 redesign replaced the DNA helix background with an animated "aurora" gradient (lime/honey/forest blobs + noise) — a deliberate evolution documented in fase-3/05; brandbook itself unchanged.
- Accessibility rules: Lime never as text color on light backgrounds (insufficient contrast); button text always Cream on Deep/Forest (fase-3/01 §4.4).
- Interior design cues (also content material): sage-green counter, oak arched niches, gres flooring (fase-5 catch-up plan, images/interior/).

### Voice
- Sounds like: warm, human, reassuring — "come parlare col tuo farmacista di fiducia"; second person singular ("tu"); short concrete sentences; light register for family emergencies (PROJECT_v2 §6, fase-2/02 §7).
- Never sounds like: cold institutional tone, "catena" style, shouted promos/asterisks, unexplained medical jargon, "la gentile clientela", useless anglicisms ("wellness journey"), irony about illness (fase-2/02 §7).
- Signature rewrite example (fase-2/02 §9): ❌ "Farmacia Vitalis informa la gentile clientela della disponibilità del servizio locker H24." → ✅ "Sono le 22:30 e ti serve quel farmaco proprio adesso? Il nostro locker è sveglio anche quando noi dormiamo. Ordini, passi, ritiri. 🌙"
- Hard limits: only legally permitted content; provable promises; no therapeutic claims (see operations.md).

### Instagram feed logic
- 10 reference mockups in `images/social/` (ig-fitoterapia, ig-integratori, ig-quote-salute, ig-rituali-cura, ig-ugc-*, …) define the feed standard; each rubrica has a recognizable template; carousels open with a 1-second-readable hook; reels 20–45s with subtitles always; big number/keyword openings (countdown «−8» style) (fase-2/08 §4).

### References
- Good: **Sunday Natural (sunday.de)** — client's stated benchmark: pastel premium packaging, beige/gold + sage green, serif+sans mix, real testimonials, "trasparenza e purezza" tone. Take the warmth, not the luxury-ecommerce aspiration (ANALISI_COMPETITIVA.md §13). Also Paride's own pastel-green shopping bag brand as bag inspiration (PROJECT_v2 §6).
- Bad / avoid: Pedroni's corporate template feel; Moesa's pure pastel-mint palette (too close); Tosi's green+navy; red "da catena" Christmas visuals (ANALISI_COMPETITIVA.md §11, fase-2/05 §2.2).

### Physical brand assets (planned/produced)
- 2 bag types (standard + curated gift/cosmetics), gift card, logo stickers, billboard 4×2 pre-opening, shop-window film ("Saremo qui"), own-label bottles for preparazioni magistrali, illuminated cross, branded company car (clean style); print list tracked in materiali-vitalis.xlsx (PROJECT_v2 §6, fase-2/06 §7).

### Channel adaptation
- Constant everywhere: tagline, pillars, palette 70/30, Urbanist/Instrument Serif, package names used identically on vetrina = social = landing = GBP (fase-2/04 §6).
- Varies: FB copy slightly more relaxed, max 2–3 hashtags, explicit CTA (older audience); WhatsApp max 5–6 lines, 1 photo, 1 CTA; ads follow platform health policies; email master template with fixed legal footer (fase-2/08, /06, /09).
