# Discovery Question Bank

Capture answers as evidence first. Promote only durable, consequential knowledge.

## Company and ambition

- Why does the company exist, and what does it believe that competitors do not?
- What does it want to become in five years?
- Which past decisions, mistakes and lessons still shape how it operates?
- What must never change as the business digitizes?

## Customer and market

- Who buys, uses, influences and blocks the purchase?
- What happens immediately before the customer looks for this business?
- Which alternatives are considered, including doing nothing?
- What proof, language and objections appear in real sales conversations?

## Offer and economics

- Which products/services matter most by revenue, margin, strategic value and urgency?
- What is the natural first transaction and what creates repeat business?
- Which claims are true, provable, prohibited or uncertain?

## Operations

- How does a lead become a customer and then a repeat customer?
- Where do handoffs, delays, capacity, geography or seasonality constrain marketing?
- Which systems, data, assets and approvals exist today?

## Brand and taste

- What should people feel, remember and do after encountering the brand?
- Which brands, feeds, images, words and designs are good references—and why?
- Which are wrong—and why?
- Who has final taste authority for landing pages, Instagram, branding and design?

## Channels and quick wins

- Which networks, locations, audiences or owned assets already provide leverage?
- Which one action is closest to producing the highest impact fastest?
- What must be set up before execution can begin safely?
